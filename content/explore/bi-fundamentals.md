## Overview

Business intelligence fundamentals start with a clear metric layer — defined once, reused everywhere. Semantic models, star schemas, and DAX measures are the building blocks of trustworthy dashboards.

- Define grain before building visuals.
- Separate dimensions from facts and measures.
- Use a shared metrics catalog for cross-team alignment.
- Apply row-level security for multi-tenant reporting.
